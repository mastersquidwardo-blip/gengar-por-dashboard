Days to sell the listed bulk at my price

How a day count is made. My price is the price on the Kramaramb listing. Market price is TCGplayer's market price for that Near Mint printing. Days to clear my quantity is how long buyers take to purchase as many Near Mint copies as I have listed, counting only days when the cheapest sale was at or above my price. Every seller's sale counts. Days for my copies splits those same sales across the sellers. When my price is within 2¢ of the lowest price, about a quarter of the sellers are counted. Otherwise the sales are split across all of them. A missing chart uses the median sold-per-day of measured cards in the same game and price band. If that median is zero, those listings are treated as not selling at my price. Restock quantity is not in the public seller feed. These days are for the copies listed now. A slot that gets refilled does not end.
Digimon and Dragon Ball price column. The live floor clusters at 15–20¢ so a buyer cannot stuff an envelope with about $2 of cards (eleven copies at 19¢ is $2.09, before the $1.49 ship on these listings). The new price keeps that guard, one step lower: 10¢ still takes twenty copies to reach $2 of merchandise. Cards that are not clearing, with a market under 50¢, go to 5¢. Measured cards with no Near Mint sales in the month go to 2¢, so the buyer is paying postage. Anything at $1 or more stays put. Pokémon is on the chart and is not given a lower floor here.
