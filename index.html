Perfect Order Gengar #050

What this is

We are guessing what two top grades might sell for: a PSA 10, and a PCG gem. The card is the English Perfect Order Gengar #050, cosmos holo, and only the copy from a blister pack.

On this page, release means when the packs hit shelves — the day a collector could buy one — not a stock-market listing.

These blisters showed up with Pitch Black on July 17, 2026. As of September 24, 2026, that is about two months of packs in the wild.

Other PSA comparisons land near $541 on the low side, and about $1,316 if you stretch them. We would not quote either of those as the main guess.

Around the PCG guess, a fair band is $140 to $182. The $161 figure includes a small bump because these would be some of the first top PCG grades people can buy. A cautious read is about $110. If early buyers chase them hard, about $221.

How sure we are

Low to moderate. We still have not found a real sale of a top-grade blister copy.

No sold PSA 10. No sold CGC 10. No sold pristine-style gem. Not for the plain blister card.

CGC has already graded some copies, including a few at the very top. None of those top blister copies have sold.

The raw cards are selling, so that part is real. The prices for the best slabs are still a guess, built from those raw sales and from older, similar cards.

  4. Around mid-October 2026

    Count about 35 days from September 11. If that holds, the batch of 20 comes back around mid-October.

  5. November into December 2026

    PSA 10s often start to show

    The first PSA 10s for a new set often take about 4 to 5 months after the product hits shelves. From mid-July, that is roughly November into December.

What we are not using

We are not using GameStop or EB Games stamped copies. Those PSA 10s sold around $700 to $825 because of the store stamp, and that stamp is a different product from a plain blister card.

What would change our mind

The first real sale of a top-grade blister copy — a PSA 10, or a top CGC gem, with no GameStop or EB Games stamp.

If that sale lands far from about $686 for a PSA 10, or far from about $161 for a top PCG gem, these guesses move.

A few other things would move them too:

  • The typical raw price leaves the roughly $8 to $20 neighborhood and stays outside it.
  • Stamped GameStop or EB Games copies get mixed in with plain blister sales by mistake.
  • Reverse-holo PSA 10s of this set’s Gengar start selling for about twice the regular holo. Then we would lean on the later guesses, about $844 and $193.
  • A real sold price for the older 151 Gengar cosmos in a pristine CGC slab lands far from the rough $240 figure we used. Then we would redo the PCG guess.
