The blister PSA 10

No PSA blister cosmos has sold yet, so the $550 figure stays a planning middle. CGC has graded some copies, and none of those have sold. The PSA number is still a projection from Perfect Order holo and reverse holo sales.

Take this set’s reverse holo and multiply by how much more an older, similar cosmos sold for versus its own reverse. That lands near $487, just under the low end of the band. The planning middle is about $550, inside about $490 to $710.

What has happened so far

Grades do not all show up the week the packs hit shelves. Here is where this card stands.

  1. July 17, 2026

    The packs hit shelves

    Pitch Black blisters went on sale. This card is the Perfect Order Gengar #050 cosmos holo from those blister packs.

  2. September 7, 2026

    The grading order was placed

    Order #5011048 went in for 20 blister cosmos copies of #050.

  3. About September 11, 2026

    PCG received the cards

    The 20 cards were received for grading.

  4. October 8, 2026

    The grades came back

    The batch came back as one Flawless 10 (certificate #000348838), eight Pristine 10s, six Gem Mint 10s, four Gem Mint 9.5s, and one Near Mint 7. This looks like the entire public PCG population for this card.

  5. October 21, 2026

    The first PCG sale

    You are here. Slabs from this batch are listed. A PCG Gem Mint 10 sold on eBay for $76. That is the first public sale of a Perfect Order Gengar blister cosmos in a PCG slab. Gem Mint 9.5 copies are listed at $55. That price is an ask, and none have sold. The Pristine 10s and the Flawless 10 are not listed yet.

  6. November into December 2026

    PSA 10s often start to show

    The first PSA 10s for a new set often take about four to five months after the packs hit shelves. From mid-July, that is roughly November into December.

How sure we are

The PSA 10 guess is still low to moderate, because no PSA blister cosmos has sold. The Pristine range is a plan built from the $76 Gem Mint 10 sale. Those Pristine copies are not listed yet, so the range can move when one sells. More top grades and plenty of raw copies can still press on prices.
